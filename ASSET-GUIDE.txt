CRIMSON CUT WEBSITE — ASSET GUIDE

FOLDER MAP
index.html                 Main responsive website
assets/logo/               Slot for your exact original logo files
assets/projects/           Three editable SVG portfolio cover placeholders
assets/backgrounds/        Reusable decorative background SVGs
assets/icons/              Small SVG interface icons
assets/brand-post.png      Your supplied brand-post reference image
videos/                    Put approved video previews here

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Replace YOUR-EMAIL@example.com in index.html with your business email.
2. Add your social links in the footer (search for the social link placeholders in index.html).
3. Replace the three project cover SVGs with thumbnails for work you have permission to show.
4. Add your exact original deer logo into assets/logo/. The current website uses a simple CC monogram instead of inventing a replacement deer mark.
5. If you add video previews, link the relevant project cards to those files or to a public video host.
6. Upload the CONTENTS of this folder to the root of your GitHub repository, keeping the folder structure intact.

NOTE
The project-cover SVGs are designed placeholders, not real client projects or finished video samples. The videos folder is intentionally empty until you add your own work.
